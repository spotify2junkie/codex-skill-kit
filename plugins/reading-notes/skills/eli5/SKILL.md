# Explain Like I Am

Explain the supplied topic for the audience and purpose the user names. Infer their background from context; for an unspecified ELI5 request, assume a beginner rather than a literal child.

- Lead with the core idea or purpose. Add mechanism, examples, and implications at the depth needed to understand it.
- Use familiar analogies when they clarify the concept, and explain where an analogy stops matching reality.
- Define essential terminology on first use. Simplify language without changing facts, hiding consequential limitations, or inventing numbers.
- Choose examples from the audience's stated interests or work. Age, job title, and family role alone do not determine expertise or interests.
- For code, explain what it accomplishes before syntax. For a decision maker, connect details to relevant choices and tradeoffs.

Use the format and length the question needs. An analogy, fixed sequence, follow-up question, or separate artifact is optional. The two-panel illustrated HTML workflow belongs to `explain-eli5` when requested.
